Fill this in and it hands back a finished entry to paste into your data file. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Links are checked against an allowlist before an entry is accepted, so a visitor can never be sent somewhere unvetted. Currently permitted: youtube.com youtu.be vimeo.com ted.com pbs.org bbc.co.uk npr.org archive.org and any .edu host. Paste a full YouTube address and the video and its start time get pulled out for you; paste a direct address for an image or a sound file and it is treated as a still or as audio. Leave it blank if you have not found a good copy yet — the entry still gets a card.


    
  







  
Clips play through each platform's own player and are never rehosted, so every view is counted for the rights holder. Opening a page contacts Google — for thumbnails and for the typefaces — and the host of any still shown on a card. Video players and audio wait for your click, and the video player runs on youtube-nocookie.com. Nothing is stored in your browser. Every card carries enough description to be found again if its link ever dies.
